How much data has Rohan shared? (the consent ladder)

  1. Pay the bill, as today. The entry point is the success screen Rohan already sees every month.
  2. See earned vs missed. At Level 2 every rupee shows its formula. Tap a card to see the transactions behind it.
  3. Milestones and expiry. Fee waivers and milestones work from Level 1. Points expiry needs a statement (Level 2).
  4. Best card at checkout. Change the category and amount. The ranking respects caps already used this month. Pay, and watch the milestones move.
  5. Trust and control. One screen shows what is stored and lets him delete it.

What this prototype is testing

  • Value in rupees, not charts: "₹336 missed" is a reason to act.
  • Least data first: useful at Level 0, better at each level.
  • Honest maths: every number shows how it was computed, and nothing is sponsored.
